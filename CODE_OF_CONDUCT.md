# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, direct, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- focus on the problem, not the person
- give actionable feedback
- assume good intent
- keep discussions professional

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- spam, trolling, or deliberate derailment
- sharing private user data or secrets

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors who do not follow this standard.
